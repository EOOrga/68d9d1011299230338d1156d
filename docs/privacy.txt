Privacy Policy for Fargeaula Ops

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Fargeaula Ops is provided by FOYER 3 EME AGE CLUB FARGEAULAIS. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app lets you save appointment bookings, shipment log entries, quality checklist status, employee directory records, and local workspace preferences. This information is stored on your device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
Fargeaula Ops does not require account registration. It does not upload operations records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share your operations records or preferences with FOYER 3 EME AGE CLUB FARGEAULAIS or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operations records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.

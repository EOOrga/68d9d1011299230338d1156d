Four operational tools in one focused app.

The Android app keeps the day organized without accounts, cloud sync, or external API connections.

01

Record visits, service slots, rooms, and handoff notes for reception and care teams.

02
Shipment log

Track incoming references, vendors, expected arrival windows, and receiving status.

03
Quality checklist

Review daily controls for reception, receiving, care floors, and administration.

04
Employee directory

Keep shift coverage and internal contact notes available on the device.

Built for repeated daily use.

Fargeaula Ops opens directly into the current operational view and lets staff move between bookings, receiving, checks, and directory records.

  • Create and save local appointment or shipment records.
  • Mark bookings complete and shipments received.
  • Toggle quality checks as the day progresses.
  • Review settings and privacy notes without account setup.
